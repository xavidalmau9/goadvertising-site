Free Wireless Service for Seniors on SSI or a Fixed Income (2026)

June 10, 2026 · GO Advertising — authorized Assurance Wireless partner

For seniors and anyone living on a fixed income, a monthly bill is one more expense. The federal Lifeline program exists so eligible households can get free wireless service at $0/month through Assurance Wireless. Offers are available in selected areas and may vary by state.

Why many seniors may qualify

If you receive Supplemental Security Income (SSI), Medicaid, SNAP, a Veterans or Survivors Pension, or Federal Public Housing Assistance, you may qualify through that program. You can also qualify by income if your household income is at or below 135%–150% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines, depending on your state.

What's included

  • $0 per month — no contract, no credit check, no monthly bill.
  • Includes 10GB high speed data, plus unlimited talk and text. Data may vary by state.
  • Buy a Low-Cost Phone or Bring Your Own. An unlocked, compatible device and a new SIM card are required if you bring your own.
  • Service on the T-Mobile network where coverage is available. Coverage is not available everywhere.

Staying connected

A working connection means being reachable for medical appointments, pharmacy calls, family check-ins, and emergencies. Monthly price requires usage once every 30 days and continued eligibility.

How to apply

Have a photo ID, proof of SSI or another qualifying program (or proof of income), and a proof of address ready. Apply on the official partner page, or ask a family member to help you complete it. One benefit per household. The benefit is non-transferable.
