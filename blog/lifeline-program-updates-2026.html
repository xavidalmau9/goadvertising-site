Lifeline Program Updates 2026: What You Need to Know Now

March 22, 2026 · GO Advertising — authorized Assurance Wireless partner

With recent changes in federal connectivity programs, many Americans are asking: "Is free wireless service still available?" Yes. The Lifeline program remains a permanent federal benefit designed to keep you connected to what matters most. Offers are available in selected areas and may vary by state. Offers vary and may change.

What the 2026 Lifeline benefit includes

Through our partnership with Assurance Wireless, qualifying customers can receive a monthly plan on the T-Mobile network at $0 cost. Data may vary by state.

  • Includes 10GB high speed data for browsing, jobs, and news. All states' (excluding CA) Lifeline plans include unlimited text, unlimited talk and 10GB of data. The California LifeLine plan includes 12GB of data.
  • Unlimited talk to stay in touch with family and doctors.
  • Unlimited text.
  • Buy a Low-Cost Phone or Bring Your Own. An unlocked, compatible device and a new SIM card are required if you bring your own.

No contracts. No credit checks. Monthly price requires usage once every 30 days and continued eligibility.

How to qualify

You may qualify if you or someone in your household participates in programs like SNAP (food stamps), Medicaid, a State-Funded Healthcare Program, SSI, or Federal Public Housing Assistance — or if your household income is at or below 135%–150% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines, depending on your state. One benefit per household. The benefit is non-transferable.
