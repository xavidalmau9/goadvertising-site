Free Wireless Service for $0: How to Get Free Talk, Text & Data

January 3, 2026 · GO Advertising — authorized Assurance Wireless partner

In today's world, staying connected isn't a luxury — it's how people reach work, doctors, and family. That's why GO Advertising partnered with Assurance Wireless to help eligible households apply for the federal Lifeline program. If you qualify, you can receive free wireless service at $0 a month.

What do you get with the Lifeline program?

Through Assurance Wireless, eligible customers get free monthly Lifeline program service on the T-Mobile network. Data may vary by state.

  • Includes 10GB high speed data each month. Data may vary by state. All states' (excluding CA) Lifeline plans include unlimited text, unlimited talk and 10GB of data. The California LifeLine plan includes 12GB of data.
  • Unlimited talk.
  • Unlimited text.
  • Buy a Low-Cost Phone or Bring Your Own. Bringing your own requires an unlocked, compatible device and a new SIM card.
  • No Monthly Bill — no contracts, no credit checks, no activation fees.

Do I qualify?

Eligibility is based on government-assistance participation or household income. You may qualify if you or someone in your household participates in:

  • SNAP (food stamps)
  • Medicaid or a State-Funded Healthcare Program
  • SSI (Supplemental Security Income)
  • Federal Public Housing Assistance (Section 8)
  • Veterans & Survivors Pension Benefit

You can also qualify if your household income is at or below 135%–150% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines, depending on your state. One benefit per household. The benefit is non-transferable. Offers are available in selected areas and may vary by state.

Why Assurance Wireless?

Assurance Wireless is a federal Lifeline cell phone service provider. Service runs on the T-Mobile network, which covers 99% of Americans. Coverage is not available everywhere. There are no activation fees, no monthly bills, and no credit checks — plus free tools to help block unwanted calls. You'll need a capable device to use those tools.
