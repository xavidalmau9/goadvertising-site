Is Assurance Wireless Available in My State in 2026?

March 22, 2026 · GO Advertising — authorized Assurance Wireless partner

One of the most frequent questions we get at GO Advertising is: "Is this available where I live?" Assurance Wireless service runs on the T-Mobile network, which covers 99% of Americans. Coverage is not available everywhere, and the Lifeline offer itself is limited to selected areas.

Offers are available to eligible customers residing in selected areas. Offers are not available in all states or areas and may vary by state. The way to know is to check your eligibility on the official partner page — a city example is not a guarantee that the offer is open there.

What the plan includes where it is offered

  • All states' (excluding CA) Lifeline plans include unlimited text, unlimited talk and 10GB of data.
  • Includes 10GB high speed data. Data may vary based on state. Enhanced offers apply in CA and Tribal Lands.
  • $0 a month for eligible customers. No contracts. No credit checks.
  • Buy a Low-Cost Phone or Bring Your Own. An unlocked, compatible device and a new SIM card are required if you bring your own.

California

The California LifeLine plan includes unlimited text, unlimited talk and 12GB of data. Eligible California customers can bring their own phone or receive a free, basic Smartphone. Assurance Wireless phones are provided free of charge to eligible customers in California by phone vendors American Network Solutions or Enidus USA, or can be purchased at a low cost by other customers. Devices may vary depending on inventory. The free phone comes with standard features to help you call, text, browse the web and use apps. Models vary and depend on availability.

Bring your own: your device must be unlocked and compatible with the Assurance Wireless network. If you are approved after choosing to bring your own phone, Assurance Wireless will either send you a SIM card or eSIM.
