Bring Your Own Phone with Free Wireless Service

June 10, 2026 · GO Advertising — authorized Assurance Wireless partner

Free wireless service from Assurance Wireless does not include a device in most states. The device choice on the plan is Buy a Low-Cost Phone or Bring Your Own.

Bring your own

If you want to use a phone you already have, it must be an unlocked, compatible device, and you need a new SIM card. Check with your current wireless carrier and warranty to confirm you can unlock the device. If your phone isn't compatible, Assurance Wireless has low-cost phone options.

Buy a low-cost phone

Outside California, phones can be purchased at a low cost. Devices may vary depending on inventory. Phones are issued to eligible customers by a third-party vendor after application approval.

What the service includes

  • Service: $0/month for eligible customers, includes 10GB high speed data, unlimited talk and text. Data may vary by state.
  • No contract, credit check, or monthly bill.
  • You can request to change your mobile phone number up to three times each year. Local phone numbers may not be available in certain markets.

Offers are available in selected areas and may vary by state. One benefit per household. The benefit is non-transferable. Monthly price requires usage once every 30 days and continued eligibility.
