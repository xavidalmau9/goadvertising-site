Eligibility

Who qualifies?

You likely qualify if you're in any one of these programs — or you meet the income guideline for your state.

  • Medicaid (Medi-Cal in CA)
  • SNAP (CalFresh in CA)
  • Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
  • Veterans Pension or Survivors Pension
  • Federal Public Housing Assistance / Section 8
  • State-Funded Healthcare Program
  • Income at or below 135%–150% of Federal Poverty Guidelines, depending on state

You'll need proof of program participation or proof of income. One benefit per household. The benefit is non-transferable.
