If you qualify for Lifeline, you can get free Assurance Wireless service: includes 10GB high speed data, unlimited talk, and unlimited text at $0 a month. Assurance Wireless is a federal Lifeline cell phone service provider. Offers are available in selected areas and vary by state. Here's what you get, who qualifies, and how to apply.

What you get

Free Assurance Wireless service on the T-Mobile network includes:

  • Includes 10GB High Speed Data
  • Unlimited text
  • Unlimited talk
  • No Monthly Bill — No Contracts or Hidden Fees
  • No credit checks
  • Buy a Low-Cost Phone or Bring Your Own

Data may vary by state. Enhanced offers apply in CA and Tribal Lands. Buy a Low-Cost Phone or Bring Your Own. An unlocked, compatible device and a new SIM card are required if you bring your own. Monthly price requires usage once every 30 days and continued eligibility.

Eligibility

Who qualifies?

You likely qualify if you're in any one of these programs — or you meet the income guideline for your state.

  • Medicaid (Medi-Cal in CA)
  • SNAP (CalFresh in CA)
  • Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
  • Veterans Pension or Survivors Pension
  • Federal Public Housing Assistance / Section 8
  • State-Funded Healthcare Program
  • Income at or below 135%–150% of Federal Poverty Guidelines, depending on state

You'll need proof of program participation or proof of income. One benefit per household. The benefit is non-transferable.
